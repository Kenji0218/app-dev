## 🍫 Forrest Gump (1994)

> "Life is like a box of chocolates. You never know what you're gonna get."

**Director:** Robert Zemeckis
**Starring:** Tom Hanks, Robin Wright, Gary Sinise
**Genre:** Drama / Comedy-Drama
**Runtime:** 142 min

### Why I Love It

Forrest Gump is a masterclass in storytelling — it takes one ordinary man and threads him through decades of American history. It's funny, heartbreaking, and surprisingly profound, all at once.

### Key Themes

1. **Innocence & optimism** — Forrest's simplicity is his superpower
2. **Fate vs. chance** — the feather motif bookends the whole film
3. **Love & loyalty** — his devotion to Jenny never wavers

### Memorable Quotes

- *"Stupid is as stupid does."*
- *"My mama always said, 'You gotta put the past behind you before you can move on.'"*
- *"I'm not a smart man, but I know what love is."*

### Fun Fact

- The film won **6 Academy Awards**, including Best Picture and Best Actor for Tom Hanks.

## 🎖️ Jarhead (2005)

> "Every man has a war in him. This is mine."

**Director:** Sam Mendes
**Starring:** Jake Gyllenhaal, Peter Sarsgaard, Jamie Foxx
**Genre:** War / Drama
**Runtime:** 125 min

### Why I Love It

Unlike most war films, *Jarhead* is about **the waiting** — the boredom, the tension, and the psychological toll of being trained to fight a war you never actually get to fight. It's a war movie with almost no combat, and that's exactly the point.

### Key Themes

| Theme | How It Shows Up |
|-------|-----------------|
| Boredom of war | Endless drills and desert waiting |
| Dehumanization | "This is my rifle" mantra |
| Loss of identity | Swoff's transformation |

### Memorable Quotes

- *"I love this job. I love it."*
- *"We are still in the desert."*

### Fun Fact

- Based on the memoir by **Anthony Swofford**, who served as a Marine scout sniper in the Gulf War.

## 😈 Lucifer (2016–2021)

> "What is it you truly desire?"

**Developed by:** Tom Kapinos (based on Neil Gaiman's character)
**Starring:** Tom Ellis, Lauren German, Lesley-Ann Brandt
**Genre:** Fantasy / Crime / Comedy-Drama
**Seasons:** 6 (93 episodes)

### Why I Love It

*Lucifer* takes a biblical figure and turns him into a charming, piano-playing nightclub owner who solves crimes in Los Angeles. It's absurd on paper, but Tom Ellis's performance makes it work — equal parts wit, vulnerability, and devilish charisma.

### Key Themes

- **Redemption** — can the Devil change?
- **Free will vs. destiny**
- **Family & identity** — celestial and human alike

### Memorable Quotes

- *"What is it you truly desire?"*
- *"I'm the Devil. I don't lie."*
- *"Detective!"*

### Fun Fact

- The show was famously **saved by fan campaigns** — first by Fox fans after cancellation, then by Netflix viewers.
